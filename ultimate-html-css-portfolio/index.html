ABOUT

Back in 2020, I was a fashion design student and got introduced to web design as part of my curriculum to create a web portfolio for my fashion design projects. That sparked my interest in web design and programming and I made the decision to teach myself how to code.

Since then I've been doing various web design projects but I wanted to take a step further and so I dedicated myself to learning the profession. I am passionate about building clean, functional, and user-friendly digital experiences. With a strong foundation in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and React, I enjoy turning ideas into responsive, real-world applications. I'm currently focused on growing as a full-stack developer by learning backend technologies like Node.js and MongoDB.

My portfolio showcases projects that reflect my problem-solving mindset, attention to detail, and commitment to continuous learning. I'm excited to contribute to a dynamic development team and bring thoughtful solutions to life.
